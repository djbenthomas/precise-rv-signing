Revenue & payment

Owner and Agent each receive 50% of gross nightly rental revenue actually collected, before platform, processing, referral or finder fees. Those fees are paid from Agent's share unless Owner approves a specific charge in writing. Standard rate $175/night, two-night minimum; seasonal and promotional pricing discretion remains. At standard rate, two nights produce $350 revenue and $175 for Owner before approved Owner expenses.

Under the selected Basic Plan, Agent retains 100% of delivery, setup, prep, cleaning, bedding, kitchen-kit and other add-on fees; extra-mileage, towing and generator fees remain allocated to Agent. Agent provides an itemized monthly statement and pays amounts due by the 5th of the following month.

Storage & commercial insurance

Agent pays all management-related storage costs and commercial insurance premiums for this trailer throughout Agent's custody, including months without rentals. These costs are not charged to Owner, accrued as Owner debt, or deducted from Owner's earnings. Owner remains responsible for registration and any separate personal-use insurance; this provision does not authorize cancellation of personal coverage.

Repairs

Basic Plan remains selected. Owner's prior written approval is required for any Owner-paid repair exceeding $250 total, including parts and labor. Exception: an immediate safety emergency, with prompt notice and receipts. Covered Basic Plan work remains Agent's expense; authorized Owner-paid work bills at the stated $100/hour labor rate.

Use, term & return

Owner may reserve available dates with five calendar days' written notice; Agent provides calendar access. Storage/management base stays in California; Agent gives written notice before any change of storage location. The winter relocation provision is deleted.

Term is month to month. Either party may terminate on 30 calendar days' written notice. This replaces the one-year term, inconsistent month entries and automatic-renewal provisions. Agent makes the trailer, keys and documented Owner equipment available for pickup in California no later than the end of that notice period, and is responsible for bookings extending beyond that date. No $300 termination or nonrenewal fee applies.

Insurance before operations

Before the first rental or Agent transport, Agent provides the policy/coverage documents and written confirmation identifying this trailer, Owner's insured status, coverage during storage, transport, setup and customer use, liability and physical-damage limits, and exclusions.

Identification

References to "Hightened Path RV" in the maintenance addendum mean the Agent identified above. California governing law remains selected.
